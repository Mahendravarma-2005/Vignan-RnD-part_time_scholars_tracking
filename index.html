Locked Profile & R&D Support

Why are these details locked?

To ensure administrative security and monthly fellowship audit compliance, Bank Account Number, IFSC Code, Bank Name, and Supervisor Contact Number can only be entered once. Once recorded, they are permanently locked to safeguard your disbursal credentials.

How to Request Profile / Bank Updates:

  1. Obtain an endorsement letter or memo signed by your Research Guide / Supervisor.
  2. Attach supporting bank verification (front page of bank passbook / cancelled cheque).
  3. Submit the request to the Office of the Dean (R&D). Authorized R&D officers will update your records in the Central Admin Portal.
